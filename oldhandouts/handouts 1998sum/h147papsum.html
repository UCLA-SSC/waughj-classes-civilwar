You may design your own topic to reflect your interests in the course. For those of you who might need some assistance, here are a few suggested topics:

1. Women in the Civil War:

See and then analyze the movie "Gone With the Wind" in terms of its gender content. How accurately does the movie portray Southern women during the war, in your view?

Or

Use biographies and diaries to reconstruct the lives of No./So./African-American women during the war. Answer the question how did the war change women's lives, and was it for the better or worse?

2. African-American Soldiers
Why did they enlist? Why did they fight? What did they accomplish? Select a black regiment and follow its history throughout the war, providing the answer to the above questions.
Or
Compare and contrast the movie "Glory" with the actual, historical experience of the Massachusetts 54th.
3. Lincoln and Wartime Reconstruction
Use his speeches to trace and analyze the changing policy toward the South as the war grinds to a close and Northern victory is apparent.
4. Michael Shaara's The Killer Angels and Gettysburg
Analyze the way Shaara depicts the motivations of the northern southern soldier in his novel through the figures of James Longstreet and Joshua Chamberlain. In your opinion, is this a realistic depiction of the average soldier or the extraordinary individual?
