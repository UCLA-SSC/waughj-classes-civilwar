Final Exam
  1. Short-Answer Identification (probably two out of four) You will be asked to write about two paragraphs on each term, first identify it and then summarize its significance to your understanding of the history of the era.

  2. First Essay Section. Using specific examples from the READINGS, LECTURES, AND WEBSITE to illustrate your points, write an essay answering ONE of the following questions. If you wish to include something you wrote in PART ONE, simply refer to your answer there without repeating it.

    1. James McPherson argued: "Union victory in the war destroyed the southern vision of America and ensured that the northern vision would become the American vision. In April of 1865 the Civil War ended with the complete and unconditional surrender of the Confederate nation." How did this happen? In other words, why did the North win and the South lose? Structure your answer around a comparison of the two nations and include the following: Politics and political leadership, Popular support of the war, Wartime Protests, Military success or failure

    2. One historian has written: "Protecting blacks from violence, intimidation, and discrimination worked at cross-purposes with attempts to renew white loyalty. To engage simultaneously in revolution and restoration proved a difficult task." Evaluate the outcome of Northern Reconstruction as it was played out in the South, to its demise in 1877. In your answer (which should include an assessment of the bi-racial Republic governments, Ku Klux Klan activities, Northern reaction to Reconstruction, and Redemption) consider the ways in which Reconstruction was a success or failure on its own terms. What, if anything, would YOU have done differently if you had been in charge of implementing Reconstruction?

    3. Analyze the changing social, economic, and political role of freedmen and freedwomen in the aftermath of Appomattox. Using Perman pp. 351-376 as your foundation, consider the dynamics of family, gender, and labor and class in constructing your answer. How would you characterize the successes and failures of Reconstruction for the African Americans in the post-war South, to 1877?

  3. Second Essay Section. Prepare an answer to the question below.

    On Saturday, May 16, 1867, Chief Justice Salmon Chase opened the Senate vote on the impeachment of President Andrew Johnson: "Senators, in conformity with the order of the Senate, the chair will now proceed to take the vote on the eleventh article, as directed by the rule. The Secretary will read the eleventh article." "That said Andrew Johnson, President of the United States, unmindful of the high duties of his office, and of the oath of office, and in disregard of the Constitution and laws of the United States, did heretofore, to wit, on the eighteenth day of August, AD 1866, at the city of Washington and the District of Columbia, by pubic speech, declare and affirm, in substance, that the 39th Congress of the United States was not a Congress of the United States...but, on the contrary, was a Congress of only a part of the states, thereby denying, and intending to deny, that the legislation of said congress was valid or obligatory upon him, the said Andrew Johnson, President of the United States, afterwards, to wit, on the twenty-first day of February, AD, 1868, at the city of Washington, in the District of Columbia, did, unlawfully, and in disregard of the requirements of the Constitution that he should take care that the laws be faithfully executed, attempt to prevent the execution of an act entitled 'An Act regulating the tenure of certain civil offices,' passed March 2, 1867, by unlawfully devising and contriving, and attempting to devise and contrive, means by which he should remove Edwin M. Stanton from forthwith resuming the functions of the office of Secretary for the Department of War...whereby the said Andrew Johnson...did then...commit and was guilty of a high crime and misdemeanor in office."
