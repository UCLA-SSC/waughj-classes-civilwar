Scoring
  • There were three matching questions on each test, worth two points each, for a total of six points. To get credit for the question, all five matches had to be correct.
  • There were four multiple choice questions on each test, worth one point each, for a total of four points.
  • There were three fill in questions on each test, worth two points each for a total of six points
  • This adds up to a total of sixteen points. The test was only worth fifteen, so those who got everything right will receive one point of extra credit.

Multiple Choice
Which of the following statements best describes the compromise position on slavery in the territories after the Mexican-American War?
Leave the issue of whether or not there should be slavery to the people of that territory: let them decide through democratic elections!

Select the one event that did NOT occur in "Bleeding Kansas"
A race for the U.S. Senate in 1858, featuring a series of debates between Democrat Stephen Douglas and Republican Abraham Lincoln over the issue of slavery in the territories

When Abraham Lincoln spoke to the crowds around his house in Springfield, illinois during the election of 1860, he would have urged all but ONE of the following:
The establishment of a target date of 1876, the year of the nation's centennial, for the emancipation of all slaves in the country.

Which of the following best describes "Free-Labor Ideology"?
A capitalist ideology which argued that to be a good worker (and a good person) you did not need to be beaten. In other words, you did not need the coercive discipline of slavery, but rather the positive incentive of good wages and an internalized value system (thrift, industriousness, ambition) to keep you on the right track. A central component of this argument was the idea of upward mobility: any man could make a success out of his life, especially if the territories were reserved for free labor!

Fill in the Blank
The House Divided Address was Lincoln's famous speech accepting the 1858 Republican nomination for Senate. Using a biblical phrase, Lincoln declared that he did not expect the Union to be divided, but to be all slavery or all free.

The element of the 1850 Compromise most hated in the North was the Fugitive Slave Law. This called for federal funds to compensate slaveowners whose slaves ran away, and provided for federal authorities (in the form of specially appointed marshals) to capture the runaways in northern, and free states. Essentially, the law now made northerners complicit as slavecatchers.

The Hutchinson Family were a popular singing group in the 1850s whose reformist commitments were reflected in their music. One of their most celebrated songs was an abolitionist anthem called "Get off the Tracks", though they would not have performed this song in the South.

James Buchanan (or Franklin Pierce) was a doughface, a Northerner with Southern sympathies. Perhaps one of the worst presidents this country has ever endured (and that is saying a lot!), his inept handling of the crisis over Kansas was responsible in large part for the breakup of the Democratic party into Northern and Southern wings.

Dixie was the favorite marching song of Johnny Reb.

The first shot of the war was fired at Fort Sumter, just outside of Charleston, South Carolina. Outgunned and outmanned, Major Robert Anderson of the U.S. Army surrendered at noon on April 14, 1861. Ironically, none of his small garrison (84 soldiers and 43 laborers) was killed in the battle, but two men died of an accidental gunshot explosion during the surrender ceremonies.
