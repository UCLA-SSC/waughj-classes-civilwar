Assignment:

Choose one of the soldiers on the hand-outs and assume his identity. Consider this: it is the year 1890 and you, like thousands of Civil War veterans across the country, are going to write your memoirs of the war. You want your children and grandchildren to know what "the real war" was like and what part you played in the grand scheme of the events that shook this country between the years 1861-1865. As you review your research materials, you realize that your memoirs are going to cover the following:

  • explanation of why you enlisted
  • your early camp experiences
  • how you felt the first time you went into battle
  • what your position was on Emancipation
  • your most significant experience of the war
  • an answer to the question: Why we fought?
