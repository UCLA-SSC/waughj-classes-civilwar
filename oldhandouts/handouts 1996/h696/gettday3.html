Week 6 Handout: Gettysburg, Day 3

5:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.: Johnson's division of Ewell's corps launches repeated but unsuccessful attacks on Culp's Hill

1:00 p.m.: Artillery cannonade begins with signal gun at the Peach Orchard

3:00 p.m.: Pickett's, Pettigrew's and Trimble's Confederate infantry advance for attack
3:30 p.m.: Stannard's Federal brigade attacks flank of Pickett's division

3:45 p.m.: Limit of Confederate infantry attacks at the Angle and clump of trees

5:30 p.m.: Farnsworth's cavalry charge against confederate right meets with disaster

General George Pickett, chosen by Lee to lead the charge against Cemetery Ridge, accepted his assignment enthusiastically. Lee was later to tell him, "the men and officers of your command have written the name of Virginia as high today as it has ever been written".


The defenders of Cemetery Ridge included a brigade commanded by General Alexander Webb. Webb's leadership in rallying his men during the confederate assault won him the medal of honor.


The confederate army had an enormous manpower loss in prisoners taken at Gettysburg. Five thousand unwounded soldiers surrendered to Union forces, more than half of them during the repulse of Pickett's charge. In addition, nearly seven thousand wounded Confederates were left behind as prisoners, to be cared for by Union medical personnel. The above Confederates were taken on July 3.
