Part 2: 6-7 pages 60% of final exam grade

Choose one of the following 3 options.

1. Harper's Weekly has announced the impending publication of a special millennium issue entitled "Progress at the Turn of the Century." L. Frank Baum and Edward Bellamy have agreed to edit the publication. Unfortunately, Baum and Bellamy disagree sharply. Why?

2. An argument could be made that Julian West of Boston and Dorothy of Kansas are representative of two Gilded Age social groups: the middle-class urban dweller and the farmer-producer. Discuss the different sensibilities of these two groups as they are played out in the novels and in history. Could Populists and Nationalists ever get together? Include an analysis of the Populist movement, the elections of 1896 and 1900 and the triumph of the Republican Party in your essay.

3. Bellamy's Looking Backward and Baum's The Wonderful Wizard of Oz are considered classic statements of Gilded Age society. As such, you included them in your lesson plan for the high school American history class that you are currently teaching. Now, you must explain to your students the connections between these two books and the history of the Gilded Age. Please do so.
