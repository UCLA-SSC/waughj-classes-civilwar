Requirements and Assignments
The course will have a take-home mid-term, a paper and a final exam.

MID-TERM: Will cover first three weeks of the course.
Handed out after lecture on Thursday, January 29th.
Due before lecture on Thursday, February 5th.

PAPER: Topic to be announced. Due before lecture, Thursday, February 26th.

FINAL: Will cover weeks four through ten and will consist of terms and essay questions.

Extra Credit Option
The movie GLORY will be shown in conjunction with this class, the date to be announced well in advance. If you attend this movie and hand in a short (2 paragraph) written assignment the following week (questions to be handed out after the film) you will be eligible for two points of extra credit. (One point for attendance and one point for the assignment). The assignment will be handed back in the last week of class and will indicate whether or not you are eligible for the credit. All students of 147A are welcome to attend the film without participating in the extra credit option.

You must attend the movie with the class to receive the extra credit: NO EXCEPTIONS TO THIS RULE!
