History 147A Paper

You are a volunteer soldier of the Civil War. You enlisted in June of 1861 for three years, and you are in one of the "fighting regiments" of the war. It is now December of 1863, and your unit has finally settled into winter camp. For a while, at least, there will be peace. But you have an important decision to make. Even though you have six months to go before your enlistment time runs out, your regimental officers are urging you and your comrades to re-enlist for another three years, or until the war ends, if it ever does. Along with the usual patriotic speeches, they say the government will pay up to $2,000 in bounties and bonuses, plus give you a generous furlough (leave) before you see action again. (Southern soldiers did not have the same set of inducements as northern soldiers). To help you make this important decision, you decide to write a letter to your wife, that reflects on your wartime experiences and reveals your thoughts not only on your own future, and that of your families', but of your country's as well. At the end of the letter, you reveal your decision: will you stay or will you go home?

What do you have to do for this paper?

1. Create a believable Civil War soldier, based on your historical research, using only the sources from 147A.
2. He can be Union or Confederate.
3. You must be accurate as to identification: ie
Capt. Thomas Halsey
Company E
11th Regiment
New Jersey Volunteers
First Brigade
Second Division
Third Corps
Army of the Potomac.
4. Please, when your soldier discusses some of the battles he has fought in, and what his experiences were, be sure your information is correct. Do not, for example, place a soldier who is in the Army of the Potomac or the Army of Northern Virginia at Shiloh or Vicksburg, or conversely place a soldier who is with the Army of the Tennessee or the Army of Tennessee at Antietam or Gettysburg.
5. Remember that Civil War soldiers were literate, and like the majority of citizens in the country, passionately interested in politics. He will be sure to offer his opinions on politicians and the running of the government.

Which sources should you use to research and write this paper?

1. Lectures on soldiers (Feb 3,5,10,12,17,19). Feel free to include what you like about songs, camp life, uniforms, food, etc. to give color and interest to your paper.

2. Soldiers Gallery on 147A Website "Summaries and Pictures" "Primary Documents" "Links"

3. Handouts for weeks 4-8 on 147A Website

4. Background material from Paludan, Thomas, McPherson, as needed

NB: You may create a soldier who is a woman or an African-American. If you choose to do so, you must adjust the above assignment. The woman would not be writing to her wife, but to her parents. The black soldier (perhaps from the 54th Massachusetts) would not have enlisted in June of 1861, but in the first months of 1863.
