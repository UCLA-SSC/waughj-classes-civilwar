The Ku Klux Klan: Founded by Nathan Bedford Forrest. One of many organizations associated with organized terrorism in the South during Reconstruction. Made North resistant to reestablishment of Southern control, even though Reconstruction was failing. Focused on anti-black violence, particularly voter terrorism. Soemtimes called the "Auxiliary arm of the Democratic party". Drew its membership chiefly from poor whites and yeoman farmers. Most were confederate veterans, used hit and run guerrilla tactics. Known as the "Invisible Empire of the South". The Klansmen dressed in all white, and rode white horses when spreading their terror.

The Only answer to control of Klan was martial law and use of troops. One governor, Arkansas' Powell Clayton, managed to so, and broke the back of the Klan in Arkansas. But other governors were not willing to act, and asked the federal government for help. By 1870, Federal government had taken action, and passed two "Force Acts", which imposed heavy penalties on the activities the Klan engaged in, and authorized the President to use the military when necessary to enforce the fifteenth amendment. By 1872 the Klan's power was broken, and they had almost disappeared (though they re-emerged again later). But the end of the Klan was not the end of organized violence, violence which which played an important role in bringing the overthrow of the radical regimes and perpetuating the Southern system.
