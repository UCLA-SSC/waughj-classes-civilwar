History 147A Midterm

Answer ONE of the following questions: (worth 30 points)

1. Many people think that the Kansas-Nebraska Act of 1854 truly made the Civil War an "irrepressible" conflict. Do you? Historian Don Fehrenbacher writes: "Southern attitudes throughout the Kansas controversy demonstrate the intensely reactive nature of southern sectionalism. That is, the South did not so much respond to the Kansas-Nebraska issue itself as react to the northern response to the issue...." Expand on his remark, tracing the consequences of the Act in the Kansas territory, using examples from the lectures, your reading assignments, the website weekly handouts, and the "Causes" gallery on the website to support your answer.

2. In reading the Stampp book on the causes of the Civil War you undoubtedly found some of the "causes" more compelling than others. Select the ONE chapter from The Causes of the Civil War that you found LEAST convincing and critique it. Your critique must demonstrate a thorough knowledge of the argument, and you must offer your own interpretation of what caused the war. Use and cite materials from the lectures, website, and other reading assignments in your answer.

3. Southerners believed that the north was conspiring to deprive them of their liberties. Northerners were convinced that a southern slave power was plotting the triumph of slave over free labor. Do you think both were delusional or both had some basis in fact for their beliefs? To support your argument, download from the website galley "Causes" one document each from the Northern and Southern side which seems best to illustrate your point. One such example might be William Sewards' "Irrepressible Conflict Speech" with Jefferson Davis' response. Or George Fitzhugh's "Slavery Justified" juxtaposed against Charles Sumners' "The Crime Against Kansas." Analyze the documents you select carefully and thoroughly. Staple them to your exam.

4. This question is for those students who enjoy analyzing politics and politicians. Describe the American political nation as the 1850s unfolded. Concentrate especially on the issues and personalities that accounted for the rise of the Republican Party from 1854 onward to the election of 1860. Do you agree or disagree with the historians (from the Stampp reading) who accuse the political leaders of the era of bringing on the war by their sheer incompetence? Please use the wealth of material on the website (Causes Gallery and weekly handouts) to support your contentions.
