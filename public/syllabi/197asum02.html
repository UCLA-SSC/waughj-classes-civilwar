UCLA at Gettysburg

This course offers students an opportunity to study intensively the political, social, economic, cultural, and military history of Gettysburg, Pennsylvania first through lectures and discussions on the UCLA campus, and then on a field trip to Gettysburg where students will visit the town, the college, and the battlefield. Guest lecturers, archival work and on-site visits led by specialists are planned to enhance and enrich students¹ knowledge of Gettysburg¹s civil war history. Several days of touring civil war sites in and around Washington D.C. are planned, including trips to Harpers Ferry and Richmond, Virginia.

REQUIREMENTS, ASSIGNMENTS AND GRADING:

Students are required to keep a journal for all six weeks. Guidelines will be forthcoming. Journals must be submitted (after rewriting and revising) on or before August 31th. Grades will be submitted at the end of the first week of September.

Students may earn independent study units by writing a research paper (10-15 pages); an annotated portfolio of photographs taken at Gettysburg with a substantial essay; or by making a film of the trip, with an explanatory essay.

Grades are based on attendance, classroom and field trip participation, and journals.

All students will be required to spend a few hours of research in a selected Gettysburg archive, memory site or library
