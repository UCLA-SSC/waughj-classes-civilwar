THE CIVIL WAR ERA IN THE UNITED STATES, 1850-1868

History 139A examines the era of the American Civil War and Reconstruction (1850-1868) with emphasis on the years from 1861-1865. The course combines lectures, readings, slides, handouts, music, website, and films to study the causes of the war, the war itself, and the aftermath, known as Reconstruction. Students are exposed through multi-media to the sheer drama and tragedy of the conflict. By this method, they appreciate emotionally, and intellectually, exactly how the war affected both ordinary and prominent Americans living in the period. The goal? To educate UCLA students about the vast scope and the long-lived consequences of this bloody conflict in all of its dimensions ­ social, cultural, military, political, and economic.
