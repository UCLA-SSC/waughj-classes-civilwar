Some tips on writing academic research papers, courtesy of Prof. Joan Waugh, UCLA Dept. of History

The Five Steps:

  • Mystery/Problem/Question(s) Posed
  • Evidence/Sources
  • Hypothesis/Argument
  • Solutions (How are you using evidence?)
  • Significance/Conclusion

    In the Introduction, the Reader Should Know:

  • Your argument
  • How you are going to prove your argument
  • Definition of important concepts
  • What body of work are you drawing on?
  • Where does your work fit in?

    Throughout the Paper, Work on the Following:

  • A good title, one that reflects thought and care
  • Smooth transitions, your paper should "flow"
  • Check for spelling and grammar
  • Use Chicago Manual of Style for citation method
  • A list of works cited (bibliography) at the end

    Some Suggestions:

  • Break up your paper into "chapters" with titles
  • Use quotes to illustrate your points
  • Do NOT use quotes in place of your own writing and thinking
  • Always ask yourself: What is the point of this statement?

    Additional Thoughts:

  • Give yourself enough time to reflect and re-write. Don't write at the last minute.
  • Be clear and concise. Short, simple words and sentences are better than long and fancy. Use the active voice.
