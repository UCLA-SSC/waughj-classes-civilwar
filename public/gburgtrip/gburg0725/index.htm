Every morning we were picked up at our lodgings by two Gettysburg College vans. Larry was one of our intrepid drivers. Emily, Laurene, Melissa and part of Chris ready to go.
Our first stop this particular morning was a visit to the Schriver House. The house has been restored inside to its appearance at the time of the Civil War. The Schriver House on Baltimore Street. The kitchen garden in the rear of the Schriver House. Note the hemp bee hive in the center of the garden.
Looking south on Baltimore Street. After the tour we walked up to the Gingerbread House on Steinwehr Ave. for lunch. Emily, Sarah, Michael and Kristen. Erik, Laurene and Melissa at the Gingerbread House restaurant.
