Roy and Don. The original dormitory and classroom building for the Lutheran Seminary on Seminary Ridge. The building was used for observation and as a hospital during the battle. It is now the home of the Adams County Historical Society. The famous cupola from which Gen. John Buford viewed the battle on July 1, 1863.
Cavorting in front of the Seminary. Tim Smith, a volunteer at the Adams County Historical Society and a renowned expert on the Gettysburg civilians led us on a tour of the museum. We could not go into the Cupola so we shot a picture out of a 3rd floor window just below it - facing east. East Cemetery Hill (to left of blue water tower at far right) and Culps Hill (to left of Cemetery Hill.) Gettysburg College at left.
View from the Seminary building looking west. Reynolds death site is in the tree line (smaller monument to right of large, square one.) Next stop the "Electric Map" at the Visitor's Center for a tourist's eye view of the battle. We returned to new accomodations in a house built in 1867 by the President of the Gettysburg Railroad. "The Appleford". Note air conditioners in each window!
