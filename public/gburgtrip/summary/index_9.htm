On Canal Walk in Richmond, looking west. The Virginia State House, designed by Thomas Jefferson, which was directly across from our hotel. Statue of Robert E. Lee in the assembly room. Photo by JW.
Don stands in front of the Museum of the Confederacy as Emily, Sarah and Roy prepare to enter. We were treated to an extradinary guided tour of the special exhibit on Robert E. Lee. Front entrance to the Whitehouse of the Confederacy. Curator Robert Hancock also gave us a tour of the mansion. It has been restored inside to its 1860's appearance. In the evening, the group went on a ranger-led walk over to Belle Isle island, site of a Civil War prisoner-of-war camp. Here our guide, Ranger Mike Gorman gives a brief history of the camp.
Looking across the island from the north side. The remnants of the wall of the camp is just behind the sign (with bushes), main gate at left. The wall was very short - it was used as a "dead line" rather than a barrier. Just off the bus in Washinton City, we head straight for Ford's Theater, which is only a block from our hotel. After dinner, we headed out on a bus tour of Washington DC (accompanied by Prof. Gallagher.) Our first stop is Prof. Waugh's research interest, Gen. U. S. Grant.
