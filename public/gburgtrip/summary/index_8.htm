General Barlow by moonlight. Much of Richmond, Virginia is very new... ...but there is plenty to see that is historical. This is Cary St. in the Shockoe district. Many of these buildings were once tobacco warehouses.
Students investigate the Tredegar Iron Works before entering the Visitor's Center. Photo by JW. Tredegar and the modern Richmond skyline. Our next destination was Hollywood Cemetery. Here is the 90 foot granite memorial to Confederate dead, erected in 1869. The monument is surrounded by the graves of 18,000 Confederate soldiers.
"The Iron Dog" - a well-known monument at Hollywood. It guards the grave of a little girl who died of a childhood disease at an early age. Prof. Waugh views the monument to the President of the Confederacy, Jefferson Davis, at Hollywood Cemetery. Statue of Robert E. Lee on Monument Ave. in Richmond.
