Built in 1797, the Cashtown Hotel was the first stagecoach stop west of Gettysburg. The hotel was used as headquarters by Gen. A. P. Hill on July 1, 1863 and was in the movie Gettysburg. First shot marker for the battle of Gettysburg along the Chambersburg Pike near Marsh Creek. Roy and Erik with monuments on the crest of Big Round Top. Another 20th Maine monument is at left.
1st Michigan Cavalry monument on the East Cavalry Battlefield. Brig. Gen. George Custer led his brigade in a countercharge that broke J. E. B. Stuart's charge on July 3, 1863. John Latschar, Superintendent of Gettysburg National Military Park, fills the students in on his plans for changes at the park, including a new interpretive plan. Photo by JW. Glenn LaFantasie lectures the students on the lives of Joshua Lawrence Chamberlain and William Oates, commanders who faced each other on Little Round Top on July 2, 1863.
Our afternoon tour is led by Licensed Battlefield Guide, Wayne Motts. Wayne is a specialist on Pickett's Charge. UCLA forms up. Wayne explained that we were in two ranks (rows) and several files (pairs of front and rear soldiers.) We counted off by twos ("one, two, one, two") down the ranks. Our last day in Gettysburg drawing to a close (sniff!) we gather at Prof. Matt Gallman's home on north Carlisle Street, nearly on the battlefield. (Brooks almost brought a metal detector to dinner!)
