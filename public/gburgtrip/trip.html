Each summer, Professor Waugh leads a small group of students in an intensive study of the battle of Gettysburg. The course begins in Los Angeles, and concludes with a two-week trip to the battlefield, and to with the capital cities of both the Confederacy and the Union, Richmond, Virginia and Washington, D.C. Also included in the 2004 program are several day trips, including visits to Harpers Ferry, West Virginia, Antietam National Military Park in Maryland, and the beautifully preserved Civil War town of Frederick, MD, site of the National Museum of Civil War Medicine.
