Roy and Erick read up on Cashtown's role in the battle. Martin Sheen as Robert E. Lee prepares for the scene in front of the Cashtown Hotel. From a photograph on display in the hotel. Sam Elliot, aka Gen. John Buford, at the Cashtown Hotel during the filming of the movie Gettysburg.
Now we followed Gen. Heth's advance from Cashtown on July 1, 1863 to the site of the first shot marker along the Chambersburg Pike near Marsh Creek. The first shot marker was hard to find. It was in someone's backyard, embedded in a hedge near the Chamersburg Pike. Erik stands next to the marker. Looking back toward Cashtown on the Chambersburg Pike from the first shot marker. Marsh Creek is at the bottom of the hill. Cavalry Lt. Jones fired his carbine at a Confederate officer who was approaching on the road at the head of a column.
Lt. (at the time) Jones saw the historical value of firing the first shot in what appeared to be an approaching battle, so he borrowed one of his men's carbines and fired. The monument was erected with his own funds after the war. Statue of Gen. Henry Slocum on Stevens Knoll between Culps and Cemetery Hill. Now the blitzers hiked to the top of Big Round Top to find the other monument to the 20th Maine. They found it at left.
