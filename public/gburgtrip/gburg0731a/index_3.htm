Monument marking the position of the 20th Maine on Big Round Top following their famous fight on Little Round Top. Monument to the 119th PA on Big Roundtop near the 20th Maine monument. Monument to the 5th Pennsylvania Reserves on Big Round Top.
12th Pennsylvania Reserves on Big Round Top. View from the north end of the East Cavalry Battlefield where J. E. B. Stuart faced Union Gen. Gregg on July 3, 1863. The Confederate positions were in the woods at right (along Cress Ridge.) Rummel farm viewed from the west. The clash started when Confederates near the Rummel barn fired on Federal Cavalry approaching from the southwest (extreme right.) Cress Ridge is on the left of the photo.
Rummel farm viewed from the south (Federal point of view.) Cress ridge is behind it. When the fight here was over Farmer Rummel found 30 dead cavalry horses in the lane bordering the right of this field. Engraving of the action surrounding the Rummel farm. Mounument to the 1st New Jersey Cavalry near the Rummel farm. When the fighting here approached a stalemate, Stuart launched an all-out assault on the Federal position to the southeast.
