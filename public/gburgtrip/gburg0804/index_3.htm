After dinner, we headed out on a bus tour of Washington DC (accompanied by Prof. Gallagher.) Our first stop is Prof. Waugh's research interest, Gen. U. S. Grant. Gen. Grant. Relief on the pedestal of the Grant monument.
Artillery limber and gun at the full gallop. Part of the Grant memorial. Detail of soldiers on the limber. Sunset over the reflecting pool with the mall and the Washington Monument in the distance. Taken from the Grant Memorial.
The Capital at dusk taken from the U. S. Grant Memorial. Our group sets off to climb the capital steps. I forgot to take the lens cap off my camera, so don't expect any breathtaking panoramas at sunset from up here. But I did manage to get a shot of the Capital Dome.
