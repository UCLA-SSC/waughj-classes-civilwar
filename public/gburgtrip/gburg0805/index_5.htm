Col. & Mrs. Lee's bedroom at Arlington. Bedroom of Mary Lee, Robert E. Lee's eldest daughter. Bedroom shared by Custis, Rooney, and Robert, Jr. Lee.
Heading back to Washington from Arlington, I came across a statue of Maj. Gen. James G. McPherson who was killed at Atlanta July 22, 1864. At one point during the afternoon I visited "the castle" on the Mall, headquarters of the Smithsonian Institution. James Smithson, founder of the Smithsonian, is interred just inside the front doors.
I visited the National Air and Space Museum. Here is the "Spirit of St. Louis" NASA's X-15, the frst winged aircraft to achieve Mach 4, 5, & 6 and to operate at altitudes above 30,500 meters (100,000 feet) I also visited the National Art Gallery. Here is a painting of civil war soldiers by Winslow Homer.
