Statue of George Washington (a famous Virginian) on the state house grounds. It was a popular practice for newly formed regiments of Confederate soldiers to pose for their photograph in front of this statue. Detail of the Washington statue. Famous statue of George Washington in the Virginia State House rotunda by the french sculpter, Jean-Antoine Houdon. It was carved from Carrera marble in 1796 and was done from life.
