Students and Gerald Bennett in front of the Gettysburg Hotel in the diamond. Photo by JW. The Gettysburg "diamond" or town square, viewed from Carlisle Street (facing south.) The David Wills house on "the Diamond" (or town square.) Wills was a local attorney and President Lincoln stayed in the house (note bunting on 2nd floor window) when he gave the Gettysburg Address.
Wills house in 1885, as it looked at the time of the battle. A cannonball embedded in the side of a building on the south side of York Street near the diamond. Abraham Lincoln and "a typical tourist" on the diamond in front of the Wills house. You can have your name engraved on a brick - look for "Joan Waugh"!
Sarah and Kristen check out the statue. Baltimore St. looking south from the diamond. Note the Adams County Court House steeple. Baltimore St. looking south from the diamond, ca. 1880.
