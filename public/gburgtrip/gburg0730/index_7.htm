Christ Evangelical Lutheran Church on Chambersburg was converted to a hospital on July 1, 1863. Christ Evangelical Lutheran Church about 1885 as it appeared at the time of the battle. The buildings on either side still exist. The home of John and Martha Scott and Martha's sister Mary McAllister on Chambersburg Street, directly across from the Lutheran Church. Mary volunteered as a nurse at the church, which was being used as a hospital.
The Scott-McAllister house ca. 1885. Mary McAllister is seen standing by the open doorway to her general merchandise store operated on the 1st floor. After lunch, most of the group took in a ranger talk on Cemetery Ridge - "The Monuments of Gettysburg" Photo by JW. Chris and I didn't get to go on the walk... we buried ourselves in the National Park Service Library in the Cyclorama building to pursue our research topics.
Roy, Chris, Melissa, Laurene and I went to dinner at O'Rourke's. Here is Roy in his new Dirty Billy Confederate slouch hat. Chris Bates, our indomitable TA, after a hard day at the photocopier in the NPS Library. Laurene at O'Rourkes.
