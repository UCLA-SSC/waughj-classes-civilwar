J.E.B. Stuart grave. Detail from the Stuart monument. Last resting place of President John Tyler. Hollywood Cemetery.
Grave of President James Monroe. Tyler's grave is directly behind it. The cemetery overloooks the James River and Belle Isle. A fine example of late 19th century funerary art.
Grave of Fitzhugh Lee, nephew of Robert E. Lee. Known as "the laughing cavalier", he took over command of the decimated cavalry corps of the ANV in January, 1865. He died in 1905. A row of mausoleums near the Jefferson Davis plot. The Jefferson Davis plot in Hollywood Cemetery.
