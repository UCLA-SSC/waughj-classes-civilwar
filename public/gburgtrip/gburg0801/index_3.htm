Our last day in Gettysburg drawing to a close (sniff!) we gather at Prof. Matt Gallman's home on north Carlisle Street, nearly on the battlefield. (Brooks almost brought a metal detector to dinner!) Chips n' Dips. At Prof. Gallman's BBQ dinner.
The professors attend to the burgers. Dinner was great! After dinner, a group decides to experience sundown over the battlefield from Barlow's Knoll. Here we are hiking north out of town.
