A little calf. (Has nothing to do with Pickett's Charge, but isn't it cute?) Wayne discusses the positions of the artillery and infantry prior to the assult from a position east of the Virginia Monument. Keith listens - General Meade's statue and the angle in the distance.
Motts took us to a swale just south of the Memorial that was nearly half-way across the field but was completely out of sight of the Federal positions on Cemetery Ridge. Pickett's division formed here. UCLA formed up here as well. He explained that we were in two ranks (rows) and several files (pairs of front and rear soldiers.) We counted off by twos ("one, two, one, two") down the ranks. Wayne then drilled us, first marching forward, then marching by the right flank (shown here). When we turned, we had to "double up", forming a column of ranks, each rank with four soldiers.
We stepped forward out of the swale into the full view of the Federal position. Keith and Erik about to cross the Emmitsburg Road. UCLA assaults the angle once again...
