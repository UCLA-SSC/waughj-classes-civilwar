A sword that was believed to be carried by one of John Brown's men in his attack on settlers at Pottawatomie Creek, Kansas. John Brown fired this Sharps model 1853 slant breech carbine during the raid on Hapers Ferry. Pieces of the scaffording used to hang John Brown.
Engine house doors and the sledgehammer used by marines to break down the doors. The trail to upper Harpers Ferry. These stone steps existed at the time of the Civil War. Photo of the steps taken in 1865.
Time for lunch. Now you can see who chickened out of the Maryland Heights hike! North side of High Street looking down the hill. The Harper House is the oldest surviving structure in Harpers Ferry. It is restored as an 1850's boarding house.
