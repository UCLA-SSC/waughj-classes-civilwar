The next day we took a bus to Harpers Ferry. Our nice weather turned bad - it rained most of the time we were there. The hardier souls of the group immediately marched over the Potomac to hike to the top of Maryland Heights. Harpers Ferry from the Maryland side (taken by one of the hardy souls.) Photo by Roy.
It looked like a lot of work - so this photographer stayed in town... View from Harpers Ferry of the Potomac and piers from an old bridge, looking east. John Brown's Fort at Harpers Ferry.
Early sketch of the raid on "John Brown's Fort" Capt. Robert E. Lee in command, assisted by J. E. B. Stuart. Shenandoah Street looking west. Painting of a vengeful John Brown in the museum at Harpers Ferry.
