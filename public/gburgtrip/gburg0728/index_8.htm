North Carolina monument in McMillan Woods. View of Cemetery Ridge from the North Carolina monument. This was the jump-off position for Pettigrew's troops. Tennessee monument just south of the North Carolina monument on Seminary Ridge.
11th Mississippi regimental monument, part of Davis' brigade. One of the few Confederate monuments to be added recently. Located near the North Carolina monument on Seminary Ridge. Florida monument on Seminary Ridge. The group approaches the Spangler farm from the west.
Spangler farm. Kemper's brigade formed to the right (south) of the house and Garnett's to the left. Armistead's brigade were arrayed in front of the tree line to the far left of the picture (behind Garnett.) Some inhabitants of the Spangler farm come to investigate us. Note Gen. Meade's statue in the distance on Cemetery Ridge. View from the Spangler barn to the north. Armistead's troops were along the treeline to the right of the picture. Garnett's were a hundred yards in front of them.
