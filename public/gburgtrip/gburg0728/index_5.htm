45th PA Inf. monument on the slopes of Culp's Hill. 145th NY (foreground) and 5th Connecticut monuments on Culp's Hill above Spangler's Spring. Low mound at right extending into the distance is the remains of earthworks constructed by the 12th Corps troops on Culp's Hill. Looking northwest toward the swale.
Position of the 1st brigade of the 1st division of the 12th Corps on Culp's Hill. The brigade had to retake their own trenches on the morning of July 3. 123rd New York monument on Culp's Hill. 29th Pennsylvania Inf. monument on Culp's Hill near the swale.
View across the swale on Culp's Hill toward Greene's positions, looking north. Statue of Brig. Gen. John Geary. Geary was ordered to the support of Sickles on July 2, but his troops got lost and never arrived. Greene's brigade's position (to left - facing north) with entrenchments. Swale is to the right (facing southeast.).
