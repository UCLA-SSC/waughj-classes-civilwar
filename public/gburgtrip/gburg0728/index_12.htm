59th New York. (Codori farm and Wright marker in the distance.) The 59th NY was one of the units opposing Wright on July 2, 1863. Monument to Brown's Battery B, Rhode Island Artillery. Some of Brown's cannons were captured by Wright's Georgians on July 2, 1863. 7th Michigan Inf. near Wright's penetration.
69th Pennsylvania Inf. Codori farm in background. 13th Vermont Inf. on Cemetery Ridge. This regiment was brought south to countercharge Wright. Brig. Gen. Alexander Webb, brigade commander of the "Philadelphia Brigade" on Cemetery Ridge. Gen. Meade's statue is in the background as is the Cylclorama building.
