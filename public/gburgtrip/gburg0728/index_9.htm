UCLA students in the area occupied by Garnett, ready to make the charge. Spangler farm (facing south.) The Virginia Memorial on Seminary Ridge. Robert E. Lee and Traveler top the monument.
Robert E. Lee on the Virginia Memorial. Gen. Robert E. Lee faces Cemetery Ridge and the equestrian statue of Gen. George Meade. Wright's Brigade began their assault on July 2 where the Virginia Memorial stands now. They followed the same path that Pickett's division followed the following day, briefly penetrating the Federal lines just south of the copse of trees.
View looking east from 200 yards east of the Virginia Memorial. Pickett's division was arrayed south from here, bisected by the Spangler farm in the distance. Following Pettigrew's right toward Cemetery Ridge. Codori barn and Pennsylvania Memorial in the distance. Codori farm from the west.
