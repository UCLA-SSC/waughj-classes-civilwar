Laurene, Emily and Prof. Waugh scramble down using a rope. Rock Creek looking west. Johnson's Division splashed across the creek late in the afternoon of July 2, 1863 in their assault on Culp's Hill. Safely across Rock Creek, now the group has to get over a barbed wire fence. (No, barbed wire wasn't a hazard encountered by Johnson's men - it wasn't invented until after the Civil War.)
Prof. Waugh goes "over the top" Continuing in the path of Johnson's soldiers, the group crosses a cow pasture on the way to the foot of Culp's Hill. View of the approach to Culp's Hill by Johnson's right flank.
Over the last hurdle as the group reaches East Confederate Ave. just north of Culp's Hill. We had been following in the footsteps of Jones' brigade of Virginians. Trees killed by the fighting on Culp's Hill. Note lack of undergrowth.
