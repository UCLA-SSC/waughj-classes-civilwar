Dead trees in front of Greene's Brigade's position on Culp's Hill. Looking up Culp's Hill toward Greene's position today. 28th PA monument at the base of Culp's Hill near Rock Creek. The regiment was deployed as skirmishers on July 2, 1863.
Stone wall as it ascends the southwestern face of Culp's Hill toward Greene's position. Spangler's Meadow at the southeastern side of Culp's Hill. Johnson's men sparred with the Federals across this field, using the stone wall in the foreground as cover. Indiana monument in the distance. 27th Indiana Infantry monument near Spangler's Spring. With the 2nd Mass., the 27th IA charged the Confederate works on the morning of July 3 and suffered tremendous losses.
Night of July 2: "It seems Captain Selfridge of Company H had taken some of his men's canteens and gone on ahead to Spangler's Spring to fill them, when he discovered "Johnnies" also there filling their canteens." 48th PA. 107th New York Infantry monument near Spangler's Spring. 1st Maryland Inf. Regiment monument on the slopes of Culp's Hill.
