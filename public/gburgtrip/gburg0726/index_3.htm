The Iron Brigade consisted of the 2nd, 6th, and 7th Wisconsin, 24th Michigan, and 19th Indiana. Statue of civilian John Burns, a Gettysburg resident who joined the Iron Brigade for the first day's battle and was wounded three times. Burns was over 70 years of age at the time.
