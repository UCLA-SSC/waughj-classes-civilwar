Monument to Gen. Abner Doubleday ("of baseball fame" - not!) on Reynolds Ave. Gen. Doubleday took command of the 1st Corps after Reynolds' death but was subsequently replaced by Meade after July 1. Monument to the 19th Indiana of the Iron Brigade in McPherson's Woods. Willougby Run near McPherson's Woods looking north. Gen. Archer was captured near here. There was knee-deep water in the run on the day of the battle.
Willoughby Run looking south from same location. Willoughby Run near McPherson's Woods taken shortly after the battle. Prof. Gallagher reads an account of action that occurred in McPherson's Woods to UCLA students.
Recent monument to the 26th North Carolina Regiment, part of Pettigrew's brigade. This regiment and those of the Iron Brigade suffered terrific casualties on July 1. The 26th later participated in Pickett's Charge. Monument to the 2nd Wisconsin, Iron Brigade, in McPherson's Woods. The 7th Wisconsin monument on the northern edge of McPherson's Woods.
