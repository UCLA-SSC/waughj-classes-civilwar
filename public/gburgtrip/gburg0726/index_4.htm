150th Pennsylvania Infantry regiment monument. Near McPherson's farm on Meredith Ave. Monument to Gen. John Buford whose cavalry fought a delaying action until the 1st Corps arrived on the field. The cannon at far left fired the opening cannon shot of the battle. Looking west toward Herr Ridge. The four cannon guarding the base of the statue belonged to Horse Battery "A" 2nd U.S. Artillery. This piece was the opening gun of the battle fired from this spot under the personal direction of Gen. Buford, July 1, 1863.
Detail of Gen. John Buford. Maj. Gen. John Reynolds. Killed at Gettysburg, July 1, 1863. Monument to the 149th Pennsylvania Infantry across the Chambersburg Pike from the statues of Reynolds and Buford. McPherson's Woods in distance.
Prof. Gallagher explains the action that occurred with Gen. Davis' brigade in the railroad cut north of the Chambersburg Pike. Prof. Gallman looks on. Gen. Joseph Davis's brigade of Mississippians and North Carolinans swept across the fields in the distance from the west (left) and then wheeled to face the photographer and entered the then unfinished railroad bed. The UCLA students enter the railroad cut from the western end.
