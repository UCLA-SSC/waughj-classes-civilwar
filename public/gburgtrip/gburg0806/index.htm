The End of an Incredible Trip

8/5-6/2001

Return to
UCLA at Gettysburg Journal

Our last evening together, we head out to Georgetown to a restaurant that Emily located for us. Chris, Roy, Kristen and Michael. Melissa, Don, Sarah, Prof. Waugh and Emily. Too numerous to mention... (besides, if you've looked at all the other photos of the trip you must know them all by now!)
In appreciation for assembling an impressive array of "rock stars" of the Civil War (including herself!) to guide us through every inch of Gettysburg, the class presented Prof. Waugh with a card and gifts and their warmest thanks. Next day we gathered in the lobby of the hotel waiting for our ride to the airport. Roy's still trying to finish that novel he's been carrying around... Cooling our heels at BWI.
When we got on the plane at Baltimore, the air conditioning hit the humidity and created clouds. (OK - so I "digitally enhanced" this a little.) Something tells me we're not in Gettysburg anymore... actually this isn't Kansas - it's the Denver airport. Our last leg. It's all over. What a wonderful trip!
