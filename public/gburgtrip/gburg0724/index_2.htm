Pulling into town late in the afternoon on the Baltimore Pike (Route 97.) "It's not the heat, it's the humidity." Off the bus at Gettysburg College and my camera immediately fogs up! Gettysburg College was founded as Pennsylvania College by Rev. Samuel Schmucker and Thaddeus Stevens, abolitionist and drafter of the 14th amendment. It stood on the path of the Federal retreat on July 1, 1863.
After some confusion about our accomodations we were assigned rooms on the 3rd floor of Huber Hall. Hot, muggy and no air conditioning! Some beat a hasty path to the motel across the street. Other hardier souls stuck it out for the first night. To attest to the fact that the College was part of the battlefield, there is a position marker for the 1st Ohio Light Artillery in front of Huber Hall. Glatfelter Hall (aka Whitehouse), one of two buildings that survive from the time of the battle.
Pennsylvania Hall. The other college building that witnessed the battle. The hall was used as a hospital during the battle - it is now the administration building for Gettysburg College. Immediately upon arrival the hungry travelers are treated to a cold cut dinner in Glatfelter Lodge. Michael, Sarah, Emily, Melissa and Jonathan chowing down.
