Taps
by Daniel Butterfield

There are a number of urban legends surrounding the composition of "Taps," but there is little doubt that the song was actually composed by Union Brigadier General Daniel Butterfield, who needed a unique bugle call to signal the end of the workday. First used for that purpose in July of 1862, it was played at the funeral of a soldier in Tidball's Battery (Union) later in the year. Since that time, the U.S. army has regularly used the song at military funerals.

Note: "Taps" is entirely instrumental--or at least it was during the Civil War.

Source (Audio Recording): U.S. Army Ceremonial Band, Blue & Gray: Songs of the Civil War (Nashville: Altissimo!, 2002).
