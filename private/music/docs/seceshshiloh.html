The Secesh (Shiloh)
by Anonymous

Little is known about the origin of this brief song. It must certainly have been penned around the time of the Battle of Shiloh in 1862, and was sung by Northern Troops while on the march.

I'll put my knapsack on my back, my rifle on my shoulder,
I'm going away to Shiloh, and there I'll be a soldier.

I'll put my knapsack on my back, my rifle on my shoulder,
I'm going away to Shiloh, and there I'll be a soldier.

I'll put my knapsack on my back, my rifle on my shoulder,
I'm going away to Shiloh, and there I'll be a soldier.

Source (Audio Recording): John Hartford, Songs of the Civil War (Los Angeles: Sony Music Entertainment, 1991).
