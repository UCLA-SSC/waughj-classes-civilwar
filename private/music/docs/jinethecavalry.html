Jine the Cavalry!
by Anonymous

Little is known about the authorship of "Jine the Cavalry," though the song was certainly in existence by mid-1863. It was a favorite of Confederate general J.E.B. Stuart, the most famous cavalry officer of the Civil War, and the primary subject of the song.

CHORUS: If you want to have a good time, jine the cavalry!
Jine the cavalry! Jine the cavalry!
If you want to catch the Devil, if you want to have fun,
If you want to smell Hell, jine the cavalry!

We're the boys who went around McClellian,
Went around McClellian, went around McClellian!
We're the boys who went around McClellian,
Bully boys, hey! Bully boys, ho!--CHORUS

We're the boys who crossed the Potomicum,
Crossed the Potomicum, crossed the Potomicum!
We're the boys who crossed the Potomicum,
Bully boys, hey! Bully boys, ho!--CHORUS

And then we went into Pennsylvania,
Went to Pennsylvania, Went to Pennsylvania!
And then we went into Pennsylvania,
Bully boys, hey! Bully boys, ho!--CHORUS

The big fat Dutch gals hand around the breadium,
Hand around the breadium, hand around the breadium!
The big fat Dutch gals hand around the breadium,
Bully boys, hey! Bully boys, ho!--CHORUS

Ol' Joe Hooker, won't you come out of The Wilderness?
Come out of The Wilderness, come out of The Wilderness?
Ol' Joe Hooker, won't you come out of The Wilderness?
Bully boys, hey! Bully boys, ho!--CHORUS (x2)

Source (Audio Recording): Bobby Horton, Homespun Songs of the Confederate Army Vol. 2 (Birmingham, AL: Bobby Horton, 1986).
