Give Us a Flag
by Anonymous

"Give us a Flag" served as a rallying cry for black troops. It references the fact that a formally organized regiment earned the right to carry a unique flag for use in battle and parades. The song's composer is unknown.

Frémont told them when the war had first begun,
How to save the Union, the way it should be won.
But Kentucky swore so hard and Abey had his fears,
Till ev'ry hope was lost but the colored volunteers.

McClellan went to Richmond two hundred thousand brave;
Said "Keep back the niggers and the Union I will save,"
Little Mac he had his way, the Union still in tears,
Now they call for the help of the colored volunteers.

CHORUS: Oh, give us a flag,
All free without a slave;
And we'll fight to defend it as our fathers did so brave;
Dallant Company A
Will make those rebels dance,
And we'll stand by the Union if we only have our chance.

Old Jeff says he'll hang us if we dare to meet him armed,
A very big thing, but we were not alarmed;
For first he'll have to catch us before his way is clear,
And that is "what's the matter" with the colored volunteer.

So rally, boys, rally, let us never mind the past;
We had a hard road to travel, but our day has come at last;
God is for the right, and we have no need to fear,
The Union will be saved by the colored volunteer.--CHORUS

And we'll stand by the Union if we only have our chance.

Source (Audio Recording): Richie Havens, Songs of the Civil War (Los Angeles: Sony Music Entertainment, 1991).
