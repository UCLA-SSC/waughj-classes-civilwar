The Battle Cry of Freedom
by George F. Root

Given the tremendous popularity of "Battle Cry of Freedom" in the North, the melody had little difficulty infiltrating the Confederate lines. Although there were many Southern adaptations, this one was particularly popular. The name of the individual who did the adapting is unknown.

Our flag is proudly floating
On the land and on the main,
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!
Beneath it oft we've conquered,
And we'll conquer oft again!
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!

CHORUS:Our Dixie forever!
She's never at a loss!
Down with the eagle
And up with the cross!
We will rally 'round the bonny flag,
We'll rally once again,
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!

Our gallant boys have marched
To the rolling of the drums,
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!
And the leaders in charge cry out,
"Come, boys, come!"
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!--CHORUS

They have laid down their lives
On the bloody battle field,
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!
Their motto is resistance --
"To the tyrants never yield!"
Shout, shout the battle cry of Freedom!--CHORUS

Source (Original Song): George F. Root, "The Battle Cry of Freedom" (Chicago: Root & Cady, 1862).

Source (Audio Recording): Bobby Horton, Homespun Songs of the Confederate Army Vol. 1 (Birmingham, AL: Bobby Horton, 1985).
