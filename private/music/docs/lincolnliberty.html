Lincoln and Liberty, Too!
by the Hutchinson Family

The campaign song was well established by the time Abraham Lincoln ran for president in 1860--so much so, in fact, that his partisans had more than 20 tunes about the "rail splitter" from which to choose. Most popular was "Lincoln and Liberty," written by members of the well-known singing troupe the Hutchinson Family. Though Lincoln promised to protect slavery where it already existed, the song acknowledges what Southerners and Northerners both knew: That his election would represent a tremendous blow to the institution. "Lincoln and Liberty, Too" was actually an update of an earlier Hutchinson Family song entitled "The Liberty Ball," and when the song was sung, it was common to mix verses from the two, as with this selection.

Hurrah for the choice of the nation,
Our chieftain so brave and so true,
We'll go for the great reformation,
For Lincoln and Liberty, too!

We'll go for the son of Kentucky
The hero of Hoosierdom through,
The pride of the 'Suckers' so lucky,
For Lincoln and Liberty, too!

Then up with the banner so glorious,
The star-spangled red, white, and blue,
We'll fight till our banner's victorious,
For Lincoln and Liberty, too.

Come all ye true friends of the nation,
Attend to humanity's call;
Come aid in the slave's liberation,
And roll on the Liberty Ball.
And roll on the Liberty Ball,
And roll on the Liberty Ball,
Come aid in the slave's liberation,
And roll on the Liberty Ball.

We'll finish the temple of freedom,
And make it capacious within,
That all who seek shelter may find it
Whatever the hue of their skin.
Whatever the hue of their skin,
Whatever the hue of their skin,
That all who seek shelter may find it
Whatever the hue of their skin.

Success to the old-fashioned doctrine,
That men are created all free;
And down with the power of the despot,
Wherever his stronghold may be.
Wherever his stronghold may be,
Wherever his stronghold may be,
And down with the power of the despot,
Wherever his stronghold may be.

Source (Original Song): The Hutchinson Family, "Lincoln and Liberty, Too!" (New York: O. Hutchinson, 1860).

Source (Audio Recording): Ronnie Gilbert, Songs of the Civil War (Los Angeles: Sony Music Entertainment, 1991).
