No More Auction Block for Me
by Anonymous

As with nearly all songs sung by slaves, little is known about the history or authorship of "No More Auction Block for Me." The song was penned at some point in the early 1860s, and remained popular in the African American community throughout the rest of the nineteenth century.

No more auction block for me
No more, no more
No more auction block for me
Many thousands gone

No more auction block for me
No more, no more
No more auction block, whiplash for me
Many thousands gone

And oh the one thing, that we did wrong,
No more, no more,
Staying in the wilderness, A day too long,
No more, no more.

An oh the one thing that we did right,
Oh yes, oh yes,
Was the day that we began to fight,
Oh yes, oh yes, my Lord.

And it's no more auction block for me,
No more, no more, no more,
Auction block for me,
Many many thousand gone.

Source (Audio Recording): Sweet Honey In The Rock, Songs of the Civil War (Los Angeles: Sony Music Entertainment, 1991).
