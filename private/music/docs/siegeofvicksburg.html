'Twas at the Siege of Vicksburg
by Anonymous

As with many Southern songs from the Civil War, nothing is known of the composer of this tune--a narrative of the woes suffered by civilians during the siege of Vicksburg, set to the tune of the popular nineteenth century song "Listen to the Mockingbird."

'Twas at the siege of Vicksburg,
Of Vicksburg, of Vicksburg,
'Twas at the siege of Vicksburg.
When the Parrot shells were whistling through the air-

Listen to the Parrot shells.
Listen to the Parrot shells.
The Parrot shells are whistling through the air.
Listen to the Parrot shells.
Listen to the Parrot shells.
The Parrot shells are whistling through the air.

Oh! Well will we remember.
Remember, remember,
The mule meat, June sans November;
And the minnie balls that whistled through the air-

Listen to the minnie balls,
Listen to the minnie balls,
The minnie balls are singing in the air.
Listen to the minnie balls,
Listen to the minnie balls,
The minnie balls are singing in the air.

Source (Audio Recording): Keith and Rusty McNeil, Civil War Songs (Riverside, CA: WEM Records, 1990).
