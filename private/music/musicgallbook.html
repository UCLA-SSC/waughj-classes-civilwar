Professor Waugh's Recommended Books on Civil War Music

• Kennth A. Bernard, Lincoln and the Music of the Civil War (Caldwell, ID: Claxton Printers, 1966).

• Mark Elrod and Robert Garofalo, A Pictorial History of Civil War Era Musical Instruments and Military Bands (New York: Pictorial Histories Publishing Company, 1982).

• Wayne Erbsen, Rousing Songs & True Tales of the Civil War (Asheville, NC: Native Ground Music, 1999).

• Kenneth E. Olson, Music and Musket: Bands and Bandsmen of the American Civil War (Santa Barbara, CA: Greenwood Press, 1981).

• Roger L. Ransom and Richard Sutch, Bugle Resounding: Music and Musicians of the Civil War Era (Columbia: University of Missouri Press, 2004).
