Professor Waugh's Recommended Books on the Vicksburg Campaign

• Bruce Catton, Grant Takes Command (Boston: Little, Brown, 1969).

• Warren E. Grabau, Ninety-Eight Days: A Geographer's View of the Vicksburg Campaign (Knoxville: The University of Tennessee Press, 2000).

• A.A. Hoehling, Vicksburg: 47 Days of Siege (Mechanicsburg, PA: Stackpole Books, 1969).

• Susan T. Puck, Sacrifice At Vicksburg: Letters from the Front (Shippensburg, PA: White Mane Publishing Co, 1997).

• Duane Schultz, A Most Glorious Fourth (New York: Norton, 2002).

• Peter F. Walker, A People At War: 1860-1865 (Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, 1960).
