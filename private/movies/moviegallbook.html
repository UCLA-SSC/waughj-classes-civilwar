Professor Waugh's Recommended Books on Civil War Movies

• Herb Bridges, The Filming of Gone with the Wind (Macon, GA: Mercer University Press, 1998).

• Mark C. Carnes, ed., Past Imperfect: History According to the Movies (New York: Henry Holt and Company, 1996).

• John M. Cassidy, Civil War Cinema: A Pictorial History of Hollywood and the War Between the States (Missoula, MT: Pictorial Histories Printing Company, 1993).

• Edward D. Campbell, The Celluloid South: Hollywood and the Southern Myth (Knoxville: University of Tennessee Press, 1981).

• Bruce Chadwick, The Reel Civil War (New York: Knopf, 2001).

• Jim C. Cullen, The Civil War in Popular Culture (Washington: Smithsonian Institution Press, 1995).

• Robert Brent Toplin, History by Hollywood: The Use and Abuse of the American Past (Lawrence: University Press of Kansas, 2002).
