"The Assassination of Lincoln"

In this scene from The Conspirator, John Wilkes Booth's plot to destroy the Union government unfolds, thought not entirely successfully. While Booth (Toby Kebbell) prepares to shoot Lincoln, his co-conspirator George Atzerodt (John Michael Weatherly) has a drink, and decides not to complete his part of the plan (killing Vice-President Johnson). Co-conspirator Lewis Payne (Norman Reedus) does follow through with his instructions to attack Secretary of State William Seward (who is in bed recovering from a carriage accident), but he fails to kill Seward. The scene concludes with Booth shooting President Lincoln and escaping through the back door of Ford's Theater.
