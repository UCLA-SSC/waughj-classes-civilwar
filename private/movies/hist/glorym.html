"Fort Wagner"

This scene from Glory shows the final portion of the Massachusetts 54th's famous charge upon Fort Wagner in July of 1863. During the charge, Trip (Denzel Washington) and Colonel Robert Gould Shaw (Matthew Broderick) are killed. Some of the remainder of the regiment makes it to the parapet of the fort before being forced to retreat.
