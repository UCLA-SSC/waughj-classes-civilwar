The Conspirator: An Overview

Year of Release: 2010

Cast: James McAvoy as Frederick Aiken, Robin Wright as Mary Surratt, Kevin Kline as Edwin Stanton, Evan Rachel Wood as Anna Surratt, Tom Wilkinson as Reverdy Johnson, Justin Long as Nicholas Baker.

Key People Behind the Scenes: Directed by Robert Redford. Written by James D. Solomon based on a story by Gregory Bernstein. Produced by Joe Ricketts, Jeremiah Samuels, and Webster Stone.

Setting: Washington, D.C. and Virginia.

Plot Summary: President Lincoln is assassinated, and the nation--particularly Secretary of War Edwin M. Stanton--longs for vengeance. Among the targets is Mary Surratt, owner of the boarding house where Booth plotted to kidnap or kill the president. When she is put on trial, a young Union lawyer tries to defend her, believing that she was unaware of Booth's machinations.

Point of View: Nominally Union, though the Northerner Stanton is portrayed as an unreasonable zealot, while it is suggested that the Southerner Surratt may have been unfairly victimized.

Critics' Response: Very divided. Many critics praised the drama and pageantry of the film, others found it dry or overly melodramatic.

Awards: None.

Historians' Response: Some historians were excited to see this story brought to screen, anticipating that it would provoke discussion about some important aspects of the Civil War. Most, however, felt that the movie took too many liberties with the historical events it portrays.

Budget: $25,000,000 ($26,622,000 in current dollars).

Box Office Gross: $11,538,000 ($12,298,000 in current dollars).

Source: Christopher Bates for the History 139A website
