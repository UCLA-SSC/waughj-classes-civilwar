Gettysburg: An Overview

Year of Release: 1993

Cast: Tom Berenger as Lieutenant General James Longstreet, Martin Sheen as General Robert E. Lee, Stephen Lang as Major General George E. Pickett, Jeff Daniels as Colonel Joshua Lawrence Chamberlain, Richard Jordan as Brigadier General Lewis A. Armistead.

Key People Behind the Scenes: Directed by Ronald Maxwell. Written by Ronald Maxwell based on the novel by Michael Shaara. Produced by Moctesuma Estarza and Robert Katz.

Setting: Pennsylvania and Maryland

Plot Summary: The Union and Confederacy face off in the best-known battle of the Civil War.

Point of View: Although the story is told from the perspective of both Northern and Southern characters, it is presented in a fashion fairly consistent with the Southern "Lost Cause" interpretation of the war.

Critics' Response: Despite its length and extremely poor makeup and special effects, the film generally received positive reviews. Critics particularly lauded the film's cinematography, and the performance of Jeff Daniels.

Awards: None

Historians' Response: The film has been praised for its relatively accurate portrayal of the details of soldiers' life and of combat. However, like its sequel Gods and Generals, there has been much criticism of director Maxwell's choice to mute the issue of slavery.

Budget: $25,000,000 ($37,840,000 in current dollars).

Box Office Gross: $10,700,000 ($16,211,000 in current dollars).

Source: Christopher Bates for the History 139A website
