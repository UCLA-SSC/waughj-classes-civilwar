Cold Mountain: An Overview

Year of Release: 2003

Cast: Jude Law as W.P. Inman, Nicole Kidman as Ada Monroe, Renee Zellweger as Ruby Thewes, Eileen Atkins as Maddy, Brendan Gleeson as Stobrod Thewes, Philip Seymour Hoffman as Veasey, Natalie Portman as Sara, Giovanni Ribisi as Junior

Key People Behind the Scenes: Directed by Anthony Minghella. Written by Anthony Minghella based on the book by Charles Frazier. Produced by Harvey Weinstein, Bob Weinstein, Bob Osher, and Iain Smith.

Setting: The hills of North Carolina.

Plot Summary: Weary of combat, a wounded Civil War soldier tries to make his way home.

Point of View: The story is told from a Confederate point of view, but is less pro-Southern than many Civil War films focusing on Southern characters.

Critics' Response: The majority of reviews praised the film's art design and acting, although many critics commented on the film's slow pacing and lack of substance.

Awards: Seven Academy Award nominations, plus a small handful of critics' awards.

Historians' Response: The filmmakers were successful in capturing much of the visceral feeling of the Civil War, a feat accomplished in part by setting the movie in Rumania. However, many of the main characters, particularly Nicole Kidman, have been mocked for having an appearance that is 21st century Hollywood rather than 19th century North Carolina. More significantly, a number of historians have criticized the film for downplaying racial issues.

Budget: $83,000,000 ($100,101,000 in current dollars).

Box Office Gross: $173,013,000 ($209,469,000 in current dollars).

Source: Christopher Bates for the History 139A website
