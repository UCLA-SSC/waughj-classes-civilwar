Ride With the Devil: An Overview

Year of Release: 1999

Cast: Tobey Maguire as Jake Roedel, Skeet Ulrich as Jack Bull Chiles, James Caviezel as Black John, Jonathan Rhys Meyers as Pitt Mackeson, Jewel as Sue Lee Shelley.

Key People Behind the Scenes: Directed by Ang Lee. Written by James Schamus, based on the novel by Daniel Woodrell. Produced by David Linde.

Setting: Missouri, the site of protracted and bloody guerrilla combat before and during the Civil War.

Plot Summary: A pair of friends from Missouri are swept up in the guerrilla fighting in Missouri. One of them dies, the other marries and leaves Missouri for California.

Point of View: Confederate.

Critics' Response: Critics generally gave the film good but not great reviews, praising the actors' performances, the direction, and the cinematography.

Awards: Won a Harry award, given to the best historical film of the year. No other awards.

Historians' Response: Historians were generally positive about this film, which shows an under-explored area of the war, while noting that the plot is only loosely based on actual events.

Budget: $38,000,000 ($50,341,000 in current dollars).

Box Office Gross: $635,000 ($842,000 in current dollars).

Source: Christopher Bates for the History 139A website
