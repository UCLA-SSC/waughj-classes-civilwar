Little Women: An Overview

Year of Release: 1994

Cast: Winona Ryder as Jo March, Gabriel Byrne as Friedrich Bhaer, Trini Alvarado as Meg March, Kirsten Dunst as Amy March, Claire Danes as Beth March, Christian Bale as Laurie, Eric Stoltz as John Brooke, Susan Sarandon as Mrs. March

Key People Behind the Scenes: Directed by Gillian Armstrong. Written by Robin Swicord based on the book by Louisa May Alcott. Produced by Warren Carr, Denise Di Novi, and Swicord.

Setting: Concord, Massachusetts.

Plot Summary: Four sisters--Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy--grow up during (and after_ the Civil War, while their father is away fighting.

Point of View: Union, although in a very subdued fashion.

Critics' Response: The film was widely praised for its performances, costumes, and settings. The general consensus was that this is the best of the three adaptations of the book that have been filmed.

Awards: Three Academy Award nominations, plus a small handful of other nominations and awards.

Historians' Response: The response to the film, its costumes, and settings was generally positive, particularly since the film does not center on actual historical events or people (and therefore cannot be accused of manipulations or distortions.) Most scholars did note that the characters' outlook was decidedly more characteristic of the late 20th century rather than the mid-19th.

Budget: $15,000,000 ($22,170,000 in current dollars).

Box Office Gross: $50,083,000 ($73,122,000 in current dollars).

Source: Christopher Bates for the History 139A website
