Gettysburg, Pennsylvanie
(1875)



In the years after the Civil War, Europeans took an enormous interest in the details of the conflict (this remains true to the present day). This was from a French history of the war, entitled Histoire de la Guerre Civile en Amerique par M. Le Comte de Paris, Ancien Aide de Camp du General Mac Clellan.
