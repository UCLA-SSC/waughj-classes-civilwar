Fort Wagner: Charge of the 54th Mass.
(1891)



Thousands of unit histories, of varying qualities, were published after the Civil war. This map comes from Luis F. Emilio's very popular History of the Fifty-fourth Regiment of Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, 1863-1865, which found a wide audience among African Americans, as well as many white New Englanders.
