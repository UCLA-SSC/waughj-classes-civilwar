Siege Operations Against Forts Sumter and Wagner
(1863)



Though this map's title suggests that it shows Fort Sumter, it does not (the Fort would appear to the east of Fort Wagner). It was created by the Union's engineering corps to document the efforts that had been expended by the Northern army between July and September of 1863.
