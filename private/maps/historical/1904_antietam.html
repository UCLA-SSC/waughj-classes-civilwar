The Battlefield of Antietam
(1904)



In 1890, two acts of Congress authorized the first two national battlefield parks: Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Park and Antietam National Battlefield Site. The latter was placed under the guidance of the Antietam Battlefield Board, who were tasked with ensuring a "clear understanding of the field and illustrat[ing], for historical purposes, the unparalleled deadly fighting which distinguishes it above all others." Over the course of the next two decades, the Board produced several detailed maps of the battlefield, including this one, as well as more than 200 cast iron historical tablets (many of them still present on the battlefield).
