Scott's Great Snake
(1861)



The central feature of the strategic plan developed for the Union in 1861 by General-in-Chief Winfield Scott was a blockade of Southern ports, so as to "squeeze the life" out of the Confederacy. The scheme quickly acquired the nickname "Anaconda Plan," as reflected in this artist's rendering.
