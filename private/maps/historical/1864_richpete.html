Hughes' Military Map of
Richmond & Petersburgh, Va.
(1864)



This map was likely produced very late in the Civil War, as Ulysses S. Grant and his armies laid siege to the town of Petersburg. When Petersburg finally fell on March 25, 1865, Richmond could no longer be defended, and the Confederate government was compelled to flee.

The images shown in each corner are both Confederate prisons. The reason for their inclusion here is unclear, though it is possible some of the proceeds generated by the map went to prisoners and/or their families.
