Wyld's Military Map Of The United States
(1861)



This map, when folded, was small enough to be carried in one's pocket. It was marketed to Union soldiers and to the British public (James Wyld's firm was located in London). Note the guess the mapmaker made--that the area above "Dacotah" would ultimately be called "Chippewa." It would be another decade before the federal government formally determined that those two areas would be South Dakota and North Dakota.
