Territorial Acquisitions
of the United States, 1783-1853



Between independence and the Civil War, the United States expanded its borders eight times, beginning with the Louisiana Purchase of 1803 and ending with the Gadsden Purchase of 1853. All postwar additions--Alaska, Hawaii, Guam, etc.--would be beyond the borders of the "Lower 48."
