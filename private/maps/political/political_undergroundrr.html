The Underground Railroad, 1830-60



The Underground Railroad, a clandestine network of white and African-American antislavery activists, helped as many as 100,000 individuals escape slavery by spiriting them away to Canada. As the map makes clear, a slave living in close proximity to the North had a vastly greater hope of escape than one living further south.
