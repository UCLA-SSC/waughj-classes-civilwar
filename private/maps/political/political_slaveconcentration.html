Concentration of Slave Labor
in the United States, 1860



As this map makes clear, the Deep South (Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia, South Carolina, Florida) was much more dependent on slave labor than the Upper South; it is not coincidental that those were the first seven states to secede.

It is also clear, as countless slave narratives indicate, that escape from slavery was far more feasible in the Upper South than in the Lower South.
