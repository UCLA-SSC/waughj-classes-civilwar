The Battle of Gettysburg, Day 1
(July 1, 1863)



On the first day of the Battle of Gettysburg, the fighting commenced in tentative and haphazard fashion before Confederate Lt. Gen. Richard Ewell arrived on the battlefield in the early afternoon. The replacement for the recently deceased Lt. Gen. Thomas J. "Stonewall" Jackson, Ewell had orders from Gen. Robert E. Lee to attack the Union position "if practicable." Had the extremely aggressive Jackson been given those orders, he surely would have attacked, but Ewell hesitated. This allowed the Union army to establish a strong defensive position, anchored by Culp's Hill and Cemetery Hill to the North and Little Round Top to the South.
