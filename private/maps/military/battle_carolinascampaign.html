Sherman's Carolinas Campaign
(February 3-April 26, 1865)



The march of Maj. Gen. William T. Sherman's troops through the Carolinas--during which they left enormous destruction in their wake--brought a conclusion to action in the Western theater of the Civil War, culminating in the surrender of Gen. Joseph E. Johnston and his army on April 26.
