The Battle of Shiloh, Day 1 Morning
(April 6, 1862)



Following his victories at Fort Donelson and Fort Henry in February 1862, Maj. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant began laying the groundwork for an offensive along the Tennessee River, with Vicksburg, Miss. as his ultimate target. He ordered his army to camp at Pittsburg Landing, Tennessee, so they could await the arrival of reinforcements under Maj. Gen. Don Carlos Buell. Aware that his opponent would be much stronger once the reinforcements arrived, Confederate Gen. Albert Sidney Johnston planned a surprise attack on the Union position. That attack, shown here, sent the unprepared Northerners into a full, and highly disorganized, retreat.
