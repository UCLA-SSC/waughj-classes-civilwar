First Battle of Bull Run, Evening
(July 21, 1861)



With the benefit of reinforcements, The Confederates were able to stop the Union attack that had commenced in the early afternoon, break their right flank, and force a retreat. The Union retreat was disastrous, and included thousands of frightened, improperly trained volunteer solders as well as hundreds of Northern civilians that had come to witness the battle. The Southerners were too disorganized to pursue and press their advantage, however. This battle served to persuade both sides of the war that it would not be the quick conflict that most had expected.
