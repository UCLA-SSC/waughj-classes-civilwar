The Battle of Chancellorsville, Day 3 Afternoon
(May 3, 1863)



Having assumed an untenable position, and with the Confederates enjoying the clear upper hand, Maj. Gen. Joseph Hooker was compelled to quit the Chancellorsville battlefield and to retreat North. The battle, in which Gen. Robert E. Lee seized a smashing victory from the jaws of defeat, is widely considered his greatest triumph (though the serious injury to his right-hand man, Stonewall Jackson, was a terrible blow). In the two months after the battle, Jackson died, Hooker lost his job, and Lee launched an invasion of the North that culminated in his defeat at Gettysburg.
