The Battle of Shiloh, Day 2
(April 7, 1862)



At the start of the second day at Shiloh, Confederate commander Gen. P.G.T. Beauregard renewed the assaults upon the Union line that had been suspended the previous day on account of darkness. Unbeknownst to him, 15,000 Union reinforcements under the command of Union Maj. Gen. Don Carlos Buell had arrived during the night. With the additional men, Maj. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant was able to turn the tide and win the battle for the Union, forcing a Confederate retreat.
