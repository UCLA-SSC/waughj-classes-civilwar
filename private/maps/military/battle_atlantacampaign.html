Sherman's Atlanta Campaign
(May 7-September 2, 1864)



Maj. Gen. William T. Sherman's Atlanta Campaign brought events in the Civil War's western theater to a climax. The capture of Atlanta on September 2 secured Abraham Lincoln's re-election, and placed Sherman in a position to take the Civil War to the Southern populace. He launched his "March to the Sea" on November 15.
