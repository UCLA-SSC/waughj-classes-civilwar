The Battle of Fredericksburg
(December 13, 1862)



The Battle of Fredericksburg was one of the great disasters of the Civil War for the Union Army. Union Maj. Gen. Ambrose Burnside ordered repeated, and ill-advised, assaults against heavily-entrenched and well-defended Confederate positions. The result was more than 12,000 casualties for the Northerners, compared to 5,000 for the South. Witnessing the carnage, Confederate Gen. Robert E. Lee famously remarked, "It is well that war is so terrible, otherwise we would grow too fond of it." The next month, Burnside was removed from command.
