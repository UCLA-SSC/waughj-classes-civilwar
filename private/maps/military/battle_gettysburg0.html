The Gettysburg Campaign
(June 3-July 3, 1863)



This map shows the maneuvering that commenced with the stunning Confederate victory at the Battle of Chancellorsville in May of 1863, and concluded with their defeat at Gettysburg, Pennsylvania on July 1-3, 1863.
