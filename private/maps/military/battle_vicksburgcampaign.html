Grant's Vicksburg Campaign
(April-July 1863)



By the early months of 1863, the city of Vicksburg, Miss. was the only portion of the Mississippi River still under Confederate control. It was a difficult nut to crack--the city's guns made approach via water deadly, and swampland to the east and south made approach via land extremely difficult. Maj. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant tried a wide variety of solutions before finally getting his armies into position to lay siege to the city on May 25.
