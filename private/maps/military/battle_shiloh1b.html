The Battle of Shiloh, Day 1 Evening
(April 6, 1862)



Following the early morning surprise attack launched upon them by Confederate Gen. Albert Sidney Johnston, Union forces rallied and established a strong defensive position along a sunken road known as the "Hornet's Nest." The Confederates tried several times to break the Union line, but were unable to do so before nightfall on April 6. Compounding the Confederates' woes was the fact that Johnston was killed by enemy fire late in the afternoon; his command devolved upon Gen. P.G.T. Beauregard.
