The Assault on Fort Wagner
(July 18, 1863)



The Assault on Fort Wagner (more properly known as the Second Battle of Fort Wagner) was unsuccessful from a military standpoint (the Fort was not taken). However, it served to prove the courage and worthiness of African-American troops, thanks to the performance of the 54th Massachusetts Infantry Regiment, who led the assault.
