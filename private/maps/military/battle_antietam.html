The Battle of Antietam
(September 17, 1862)



The Battle of Antietam was a turning point of the Civil War. Though a tactical stalemate, it resulted in the withdrawal of Gen. Robert E. Lee's army from Maryland back to Virginia. This was enough of a victory to allow President Lincoln to issue the Preliminary Emancipation Proclamation five days after the battle. It was also enough of a draw to allow Lincoln to remove his popular but ineffectual general-in-chief George B. McClellan from command. This map provides an overview of the critical movements of the battle, the best known being Maj. Gen. Ambrose Burnside's repeated, bloody, and ultimately unsuccessful attempts to flank the Confederate line on the southern portion of the battlefield.
