The Battle of Chancellorsville, Day 1
(May 1, 1863)



At the start of the Battle of Chancellorsville, Union forces under the command of Maj. Gen. Joesph Hooker were on the offensive and had the upper hand. However, as the day came to a close, Hooker decided to stop his advance based on an overestimate of Confederate troop strength. This allowed Gen. Robert E. Lee to seize the advantage on May 2.
