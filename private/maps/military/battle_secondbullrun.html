The Second Battle of Bull Run
(August 30, 1862)



The Second Battle of Bull Run unfolded over two days, August 29 and 30, 1862. The second and decisive day of the battle featured the largest mass assault to take place during the Civil War, when 28,000 Confederates under the command of Lt. Gen. James Longstreet swept around the left flank of the Union's Army of Virginia and crushed it. Union commander Maj. Gen. John Pope was apparently unaware of the presence of Longstreet's divisions, and was consequently relieved of his command two weeks later.
