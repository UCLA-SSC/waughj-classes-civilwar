The Appomattox Campaign
(April 2-9, 1865)



The Appomattox Campaign was the final series of engagements in the Civil War's Eastern Theater. Following Gen. Robert E. Lee's abandonment of Petersburg, Va., the armies of Lt. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant (to the South of Lee) and Maj. Gen. Philip Sheridan (to the North of Lee) cooperated in trying to trap the remaining Confederate forces. They succeeded at Appomattox Court House, compelling Lee's surrender on April 9, 1865, and bringing an end to the Civil War.
