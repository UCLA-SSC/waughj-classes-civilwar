The Battle of Cold Harbor
(June 3, 1864)



The Battle of Cold Harbor, a key part of the final major campaign of the Civil War's eastern theater, was waged from May 31 to June 12, 1864. The most significant combat took place on June 3, as shown in this map. Looking back after the war, Union commander Lt. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant judged this to be his worst day as a military commander. Writing in his memoirs, he remarked that, "I have always regretted that the last assault at Cold Harbor was ever made...no advantage whatever was gained to compensate for the heavy loss we sustained."
