Professor Waugh's Recommended Books on the Confederacy

• Richard E. Beringer, ed., Why the South Lost the Civil War (Athens: University of Georgia Press, 1986).

• William J. Cooper, Jefferson Davis, American (New York: Alfred A. Knopf, 2000).

• Charles B. Dew, Ironmaker to the Confederacy: Joseph R. Anderson and the Tredegar Iron Works (New Haven, CT: Yale University Press, 1966).

• William C. Davis, Look Away! A History of the Confederate States of America (New York: Free Press, 2002).

• Drew Gilpin Faust, The Creation of Confederate Nationalism: Ideology and Identity in the Civil War South (Baton Rouge: Louisiana State University Press, 1988).

• Gary W. Gallagher, The Confederate War (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1997).

• Albert Burton Moore, Conscription and Conflict in the Confederacy (New York: The Macmillan Company, 1924).

• George C. Rable, The Confederate Republic: A Revolution against Politics (Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, 1994).

• Emory Thomas, The Confederate Nation (New York: Harper & Row, 1979).

• Emory Thomas, The Confederate State of Richmond: A Biography of the Capital (Austin: University of Texas Press, 1971).
