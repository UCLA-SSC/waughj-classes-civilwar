Professor Waugh's Recommended Books on Civil War Soldiers

• Bruce Catton, Grant Moves South (Boston: Little, Brown, 1960).

• Larry J. Daniel, Soldiering in the Tennessee: A Portrait of Life in a Confederate Army (Chapel Hill: The University of North Carolina Press, 1991).

• Joseph T. Glatthaar, The March to the Sea and Beyond: Sherman's Troops in the Savannah and Carolina Campaigns (New York: New York University Press, 1985).

• Earl J. Hess, The Union Soldier in Battle (Lawrence: University of Kansas Press, 1997).

• Gerald E. Linderman, Embattled Courage (New York: Free Press, 1987).

• James M. McPherson, For Cause and Comrades (New York: Oxford University Press, 1997).

• Reid Mitchell, The Vacant Chair (New York: Oxford University Press, 1993).

• James I. Robertson, Jr. Soldiers Blue and Gray (Columbia: University of South Carolina Press, 1988).

• Bell Irvin Wiley, Billy Yank (Garden City, NJ: Grosset & Dunlap, 1971).

• Bell Irvin Wiley, Johnny Reb (Garden City, NJ: Grosset & Dunlap, 1971).
