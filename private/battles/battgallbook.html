Professor Waugh's Recommended Books on Civil War Battles

• Michael Ballard, Vicksburg: The Campaign that Opened the Mississippi (Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, 2004).

• Robert U. Johnson, and Clarence C. Buell, eds. Battles and Leaders of the Civil War (New York: The Century Company, 1887-1888).

• Gary W. Gallagher, ed., Military Campaigns of the Civil War Series (Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, 1993-2011).

• Ernest B. Furgurson, Not War But Murder: Cold Harbor, 1864 (New York: Alfred A. Knopf, 2000).

• George Rable, Fredericksburg, Fredericksburg (Chapel Hill: University of North Carolina Press, 2002).

• Gordon C. Rhea, The Battle of the Wilderness: May 5-6, 1864 (Baton Rouge: Louisiana State University Press, 1997).

• Stephen W. Sears, Chancellorsville (New York: Houghton Mifflin Company, 1996).

• Stephen W. Sears, Landscape Turned Red: The Battle of Antietam (New Haven, CT: Yale University Press, 1983).

• Steven E. Woodworth, Six Armies in Tennessee: The Chickamauga and Chattanooga Campaigns (Lincoln: University of Nebraska Press, 1999).
